# Day 5: The Map

> So, today's exercise is to draw out the physical contours of your map, and then identify at least three places that your people might live. You don't need names for them yet-- we'll worry about names next week, when we give our people language. Just draw a dot on the map or maybe sketch a little "house" symbol to indicate that people have settled that area.

(I have the map for this in my mind mentally, but I haven't drawn them properly. I'll do my best to describe them down below, along with the potential settlement place that they have.)

The world is set on a planet designated by BOOTHEs as Skyworld-13, where the population has risen above the ground to escape the ground level that has turn inhabitable after it began to heat up on its own. The heating up process, however, also affects the sea and created a permanent fixture in the sky, a massive hurricane (hypercane), with the hottest part of the seas as its eye.

The map I have in mind is circular, and the settlements live on the edge of the hypercane Assaloa, using the naturally generated wind as a source of power that keeps them afloat.
