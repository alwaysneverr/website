# Day 2: The Physical Planet

> Your exercise for today is to jot down ten plot devices that relate to weather, and what you think they do to the story (for example: a snow-in can turn the mood very claustrophobic... or very intimate).

- hot weather can make people sweaty and manic, the hotter it gets, the crazier they become
- heavy rain might make for a somber heart to heart scene between two knights fighting, or between two lovers while one is about to leave on duty
- Morning Frigid can be felt while a group is walking together in the morning to watch sunset
- Plain sunny days as the backdrop to a group of kids
