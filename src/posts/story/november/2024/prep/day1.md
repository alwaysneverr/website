A/N: I am following through the exercise called ["30 Days of Worldbuilding"](https://www.web-writer.net/fantasy/days/) as a way to prepare for my 50,000 words challenge this November.

# Day 1: Climate and Variety

> The first fifteen minute exercise is to write down all the different climates you can think of-- if you need to just say a city name, do it. Sometimes "Seattle" is more evocative than "northern damp temperate climate." Write these names down in a list.

> Then, go through that list and write one or two words that describe how that climate, either the word itself or the way the place itself may have made you feel, if you've been there before. Try to stick to abstract adjectives; emotional words, if you can, but nouns are also okay.

- Rain and wet (but no wind) - Thoughtful
- Dry Heat (city I go to college to, my city sometimes) - Dying
- Filled with wind that whistles through window crack (but not raining) - Comfy (somehow)
- Snowflakey (cold, snows are like droplet of frozen rain) - Shivers
- Plain sunny day (not too hot, not too cold, maybe a bit cloudy) - Lounging 
- Cloudy (but not raining, just invoking cool sensation) - Vibing
- Humid Heat (not sure if I ever feel this) - Sweating, Uncomfortable
- Rain and wind (hurricane? but only the winds brings noise) - cautious
- Heavy rain that almost deafen you (or blinds you when you drive) - Sleepy, Wary
- Ice rain - Wonder
- Damp, sticky, but not hot (humid?) - Itchy
- Late Night/Early morning frigid (sweater weather) - Refreshing
- Mountains/high altitude frigid (jacket weather) - Romantic (if with someone), Depressed (if alone)
- Beach sunny (salty whiff of air) - Relaxed
- Coastal cloudy (salty, cool, and still bright) - Happy
