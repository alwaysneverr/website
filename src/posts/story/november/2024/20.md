Talent.

Do you believe that talent can be wasted? You must be, if you're reading this record, as this is written by someone that has clearly did the deed. Did you know that talent is like a seed? That it requires nurture, time, and care put into it for it to bloom to be beautiful. To be perfect.

But just like how many seeds lay left to waste in the dirt without any nourishment, talent that doesn't get cared for properly will rot and waste, and it will get swallowed up by the earth. To provide for their own cycle of nature.

And so I, the main character of this story called my life, is in the process of being eaten alive by this "Earth".

I've been told on many occasion by my parents that I am special. That I am gifted. That my intelligence are too powerful to be used on things like music and art.

Or hanging out with friends my own age.

Is it then my fault that I, on this age of twenty-somethings, have poor networking skills and dwindling amount of friends? Is this not the natural ending of the path that was laid out to me for years?

Alright, enough with the self-seething rambling. You're not reading this to experience the wallows of my heart. If you're reading this, then you should know me already, or rather, who I am at this moment of writing.

My name is... not important and not relevant to this story. You already know who I am anyway. I am one of the person responsible for the incident known around the world as "The End". I'm writing this for future generation that manages to survive this in order for them to not repeat the same mistake that I did.

Yeah, *my* mistake. You will not find any records by my peers who helped bring about the end of the world anywhere, because they feel no remorse for doing this, no regret. They will sooner kill you before you can force them to reflect on their actions, so forget about looking for answers for them.

And those who do feel regret has left this world anyway, by their own choice, or by force. There are no need to search for their thoughts, as they have left none that I know of.

Dead men tell no tales.

Anyway, to explain everything, I first need to explain to you about my own past. My childhood. You might think this isn't relevant to the actions that I have done, but it does.

Because everything begins there. Everything... started when my parents were visited by *the* Corporation.

-----

I was too young to properly remember everything that happened back then. But I do know this, my parents *were* visited by *them*. The Corporation. The one who is probably calling the shots in the world you are living right now.

I do remember my parents' warmth before they were visited by them. Younger me would be showered with love as they joked around with me, playing with me, even if they only do it on the weekends because of how busy they are. I still remember dinner time with everyone, where we share stories about what happened on that particular day with everyone in my family, including my older sister.

I still remember how close I am to my older sister, our bond was stronger as a sibling. I remember crying because my food was taken by a stray cat, and her sharing her food with me. I remember sharing toys with her, even if we played different kinds of toys. I remember helping her with her homework, even though I was too young to go to school at the time. I still remember her astonishment at the time, and my parent's shocked face as she told them about what happened.
