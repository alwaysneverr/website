Time stood still, while I remain moving.

In this world, what was thought to be fiction, or supernatural, has become a reality. 

It's been less than a year since an Angel descended upon my city, with his white-feathered wings, pale skin, and red eyes, and became its hero.

With his emergence, however, others that has concealed themselves in the shadows for decades has grown bolder. Criminals now begun to use their powers to wreak havoc all across the world.

Sadly, the Angel can't be in multiple place at once.

Soon, the public opinion regarding people with power began to sour. The governments began to enact laws to curb the chaos, and even hired powered people of their own to combat those with nefarious motives.

One such laws would design people with power as basically a second-class citizen.

I sighed as I looked at the frozen display. It shows the crumbling of another building, a result of a fight between people with powers.

I then turned around and walked away, making sure to not touch any of the frozen people in my way.

I used to think that this power was a gift. I mean, it's a power to freeze time itself. That allows me to have all the time in the world, to do whatever I wanted.

I can do all of my assignments in record time, read a book without a single second passing.

But now... I cannot help but feel like it's a burden.

I can never reveal to others that I have powers, what with the news showing us 24/7 about the destruction that others has caused, and the deaths that follows them.

Every time I go back home to have a family gathering, all they can talk about is people with powers... and how relieved they feel that there's no one in our family that has one.

I feel sick to the stomach every time they do that.
