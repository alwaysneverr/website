I'm running.

I'm running from all my responsibility, all the things I needed to do.

I have so much shit that I needed to be done by this date, and yet here I am.

I'm running.

I lived near this park that people go to whenever they want to have a morning jog.

I always looked at them whenever I have to go back home after a day of thinking about designs. I saw how packed this park is, and remind myself that I should probably run here some day.

That day happens to be today. I'm running.

To be quite honest, I'm stuck at the crucial step of the designing process right now. I needed to take a break.

Well, at least, that's what a certain someone told him.

"C'mon, one more lap!" She said with a grin. Bree came by to my house and kicked the door open, somehow knowing that I'm in a rut. Perhaps not answering her messages clues her in to my current state of being.

"can we take a break for a second?" I said, out of breath. I don't usually jog this much in the morning... or ever, perhaps.

"Bro, it's only been five laps."

"I'm sorry, I just don't have your physique, capische?" I said grumpily. I wore a black shirt over a tan short, while she is wearing a sports bra and these joggers sweatpants that is wider at the bottom.

"Aww, so first place winner's secret of winning is to never go outside, not even once, huh?" I can sense the smug from her tone, and turned to look at her giving a smug face. "I guess that's the one thing I have over you."

My eyes twitched. "Are we doing this?"

She takes her tongue out and starts running. "Don't force yourself to try and catch me!"

"You are so gonna get it-" I said as I started running.
