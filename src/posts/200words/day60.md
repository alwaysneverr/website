The void calls for me, as my brilliance dims in the face of infinite scrolling of the technological advancements.

My eyes fell victim to the mire of automated posts, fake outrage, and miles and miles of passionate arts, or manufactured works.

I am trapped in the cycle of consummation, and I cannot get out.

I do not want to get out.

I want to stay in this shell, and retreat from this world.

This beautiful, stupid, shining, yet chaotic, world.
