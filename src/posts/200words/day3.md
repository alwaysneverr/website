Why must fate write in scarlet?

A question that runs through my mind every single day, as I work to circumvent fate itself. To stop the blood red ink from ever getting itself stained on the book called life itself. To stop that crimson thread from weaving itself to the tapestry called destiny.

I was never the same after what I witnessed that day, where the vermillion veil dusted itself over me in a sigh, and the bonds that ties me to this world is severed cleanly as if it was decapitated.

Growing up, I started to question the reality of life around me. Is this really what I deserved? Must fate continue to run its course? Should I continue to live the life that was not decided by me?

I started to imagine of a world where fate bend itself the other way, where my life did not diverge from its peaceful way. A world where I could be happier.

And so imagination turns into ideas. Fanciful ideas, ideas that exist in the realm of hypotheticals, not based in reality. I was swept by the notion that I could work around causality, to remedy something that was neither a mistake nor a choice, and it seems like my ideas resonated with the people.

It was not my plan to ever publicize these thoughts, these wild drafts that borders on fiction instead of rooting itself in the mechanics of this world. But a part of me, a part that whispers in the familiar soothing voice of a person long gone, subtly encouraged me to try it out.

And in the thunderous applause and surges of camera flashes, all I see is red. The garnet of thoughts that overflows, flying all over the place in form of butterflies. The work is not yet over. This is only the first step to fully realize it.
