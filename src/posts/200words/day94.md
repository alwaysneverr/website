*I crashed head first into the entity I recognized as my writer's block, and I ended up getting hurt.*

Backspace.

*I crashed head first into the entity I recognized as my writer's block, and it bursts into a golden rainbow of inspiration.*

Head shakes. Backspace.

*I crash landed feet first into the entity, my writer's block, and it felt good.*

"No... this doesn't feel right." I muttered to myself.

Backspace. Backspace. Backspace.

It was early morning in the autumn season. The weather seems perfect for me, wearing my warm black coat while sitting in a cafe. As someone who rarely goes out of my "cave", as my editor would say, this is a perfect opportunity for me to be "productive" and "changes up my environment".

Or so it seems, but here I am, not even one line into the continuation of my story, and I'm already struggling.

I grumbled once more, and then heard a chuckle from the barista on the other side of my table. There's no one else here in this small cafe, positioned a little bit out of the way of the streets. I happen to know this place because I know who owns the place, and she's currently also serving as its barista.

"Bree." I glared at her.

"Chris." She said, with a smug smile. "That's the fourth grumble that I heard from you so far."

"Why are you even counting them in the first place..."

"I'm bored." She said, stretching her arms before resting her head above her hands on the table.

"Then maybe you should start marketing your place better."

This small cafe is slightly bigger than my own "cave", but it feels more homely, due to various decorations that peppers each corner of the cafe. A shelf filled with decorative coffee beans, some plants, and various framed inspirational quote posters. All in all, this cafe definitely has Bree's signature touch on it.

---

It remains frustrating, to be trapped within your own mind and unable to let out what you are really thinking properly. Every train of thought stuttering as if it was a record scratching, and it almost feel like a patch of fog is clouding my brain. I do not know if this is indeed something of a physical phenomenon that are preventing me from working at full power, and the more I think about it, the less I want to confirm it. I'm no longer the same person that can fully type out a story in one go. Every time I tried now, my mind wanders, as if it is distracted, by nothing in particular. At times, the distraction is caused by my own mind blanking at an inconvenient time. It is as if my mind is a YouTube video, buffering due to slow internet. Is it perhaps due to me refusing to eat properly, in order to reduce the weight of my body? Is it because of the raging depression, to the likes of which I've never experienced? 

If what I endured during covid can be equated to anything, I would think it is a raging black fire. There is passion lying beneath that bout of emotional distress. Anger. Impulses that are triggered because of an emotional reaction. Perhaps you can say that only 1/4 of it is an absence of emotion. Apathy. Lack of impulses that triggers me to do anything.

If I can describe what I am enduring now, it would be as if that portion, that quarter of the experience, is the whole thing. I no longer feel angry. The dominating emotion of the maelstrom that is my emotional quotient is... for a lack of a better term, *lack* of emotion at all.

In short, it seems like I am giving up.

I was already giving up for the most part during covid, but my youth still seem to burn with enough passion that I was able to get up afterwards. This time, however, only embers remain. The fires no longer burst out of me, waiting for me to open the lid for it to raze out. Instead, the vessel containing them seems cold to the touch, and it is as if there are no more oxygen for the fire to continue to burn.

I do not have it in me to get up again. Words of affirmation seems to just bounce out of me, passing through me as if it's wind sweeping through my hair. It does not stick to me, like dust sticking to my oily skin making way for acne to form in my face for the first time in years.

This time, it's like, I'm *physically* unable to get up. It took me longer to wake up in the morning. It's like my body is telling me to give it up themselves... and deep in my subconscious, I must have agreed.

What, pray tell, would you believe to be the cause? Perhaps because of the work experience you just endured this year, that makes you certain that you wouldn't be able to mesh with most of the currently open job market? Or is it the deteriorating health of your father, that forces you to tend to him daily, if not hourly?

Or is it the sudden choice to abscond from the planes of social media, who you think has turned you and many others into a brainless worshipper of the phone screen? Or your continuing stubbornness to not engage with your real life friends, because of some excuse you're not even really sure about yourself?

Perhaps all of them contributed to this state of yours? External factors of the uncertainty of job markets and the distress you feel for having to tend to not just your mother but also your father, and also your sister. These in turn influenced you to be affected internally, starting to distance yourself from people who you considered as friends, and people who considers you as friends.

But perhaps the passionate heat deep within you remains, burning its last vestiges of fuel in order to gift you one redeeming revelation; this entire rant. You have written more words in months, but you do understand that this comes because you have no other choice *but* to write. In any other circumstances, you turn to your vices, including games. You also spend your time more on reading books now, isolating yourself from social events, but you have done that already for most of your life.

But perhaps, the most distressing one is that you are now aware of how old you really are.

Twenty-five. You won't be judged if you have a child now, but you are not. You remain alone, unable to deal with your emotion, and unable to love. Perhaps this has hurt you the most, that you would rather sever the remaining things that brings you joy, intentionally or unintentionally.

Perhaps this time, you cannot avoid having to look inward and realized that you are not desirable by anyone.

Perhaps.

But-
