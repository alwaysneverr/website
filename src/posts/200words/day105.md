I realized that this block is not just like any of my previous writing block.

It's no longer an absence of idea that is stopping me, or an abundance of ideas that competes with each other and cannibalize each other to a point where I can't make sense of it all anymore.

It now has its own voice. It doubts. It discourage.

Perhaps this was already something I encounter years ago, in the days where I have nothing but my own thoughts, living alone.

But now the voice genuinely sabotaged every attempt I made on making something. It whispers into my ears and tampered my process. It halts me in my tracks. It does not get quiet with time. I can't vanquish it.

It is an infinite wave. It doesn't ebb and flow, it just crashes into me, unflinchingly, unforgivingly.

It's not a slow death by drowning as I lose my energy to stay afloat. I can't even breathe properly before the next wave hits me.

I can't even think.

I can't even.
