The state that I am right now reminds me of who I was back before COVID.

Before I move to live alone in another city. Way back then.

When I was still a young boy. Without Twitter and Discord to sustain me. Where I was addicted to multitude of games and books.

I wonder if that boy would have done the same things I do, if he has the same resource that I do. Right now, I'm literally waiting to download another Gacha that I already deleted months prior, just because they are releasing a new free skin... you should really look at the skin, though. To be quite honest, I take one gander, and my hands just moved on its own to redownload the game.

Anyway...

Yeah, I feel like I'm back to being that boy again. After everything is over now, I'm back to being that little kid... that uses everything in his power to waste his time, instead of looking towards his future.

I'm regressing.

-----

So many things that has happened this year.

So many things that I wished could have happened in some other way...

I really didn't think I would survive this endgame college stuff about a few months in. I really thought I would ruin it all. But here I am, with a fucking degree behind my name.

I really thought I would at least document my suffering through videos, but really I realized that was more of a cry of help, because how the fuck did I even think of that right in the middle of one of the most stressful period of my fucking life?

And then... my website... my *other* website, I mean. There's no going back now... I've pulled the plug on that one. I'm trying out some things right now that would restore it in a different fashion under the hood, while retaining the appearance that I have maintained for so long... or maybe upgrade it like I always wanted to, since it has been too long now that I'm using bare HTML/CSS/JS for that website.

I've finished reading *Ascendance of a Bookworm* series. All 33 volumes... with some extra volumes for short story collections and fanbooks. I can now start reading the short spinoff series that would have their second volume released soon too. The success of this galvanized me to start stockpiling other series, and even successfully boosted my confidence to read other series too, such as continuing my *Chitose is in the Ramune Bottle* reading (finished volume 4 now, onto volume 5), reading volume 1 of *My Stepmother's Daughter is My Ex* (quite the nostalgia... I watched the anime for this somewhere during my 2024 Daily Project or before), and finished *Isles of the Emberdark*! (Biggest upgrade to that *Sixth of Dusk* short story. Brandon really is on his element with these one-off cosmere novels.)

...

This really turns out not to be a 200 words project anymore, isn't it? It has become a vehicle for me to rant about my own life, instead of writing about that pseudo-scientific romance story about the creator of that machine that was featured in my short story...

........

Perhaps I should refocus. I won't end this once New Year comes. I would, however, start something else to be my blog. Another daily blog. A year off is enough for me. In 2026, I will return to writing daily once again..

I'm bouncing on the ideas of counting all of the days it has been ever since I was born, and started with that as the counter for the title of the new daily blog. I really like titles that has progression to it. It really gives me a sense of satisfaction, you can say.
