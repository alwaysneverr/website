I never really understood clouds.

Some would look at it and imagine as if it has shapes, as their brains conjure up images that reflected their inner thoughts, revealed through the eyes of the beholder as "shapes", even though these clouds doesn't shape like anything they are secretly thinking of.

So no matter how much I would look at them, none of these clouds would warp into anything that I'm thinking of inside. Because none of these clouds would shape into the machine that I want to make.

That's where I found myself recently, after my first competition win. The first of many, I am sure.

But I found myself daydreaming and wondering about the next step, looking to the clouds like some kind of troubled inventor looking for inspiration in the clear blue sky.

This is bullshit.

It was as if my first win was a fluke, a lucky break, the grand entrance without any great follow up. As if that competition is a dream and now I am faced with reality.

I need to come up with something. I need to come up with something. I need to come up with something. I need to come up with something. I need to come up with something. I need to come up with something-

I felt coldness on my cheeks. "Ow!" I exclaimed, brought out of my spiral. I turned to look and saw my rival, grinning at me.

"Yo, Scarlet Star. Brought you something."
