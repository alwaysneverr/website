When will this end?  
Will it end when I took destiny to my own hand?  
Will it end when I rise up and stand?  
Will it end when I start to make amends?  

Or will it stop?  
Will it stop as I fall to despair and rot?  
Will it stop as I face the karma I wrought?  
Will it stop as all is naught?  

When? When? When?  
Time goes on as I keep asking when  
World goes on as it continues to spin  
Life goes on as other people continue to win  

What does time mean when I don't respect it?  
When I waste it on thoughtless action? spit  
on it like a feckless abstraction? sit on it  
as if it was a contraption? a cockpit  
in the half-assed plane that is my shit  
life?
