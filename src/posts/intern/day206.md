A small goal. 200 words a day. A fifth of what I tried to attempt six years ago, in this same timeframe.

It seems doable. It can inspire not just me, but this brat that wants to find meaning in his life. Two birds, one stone.

I'll be honest, with this on, and my attempt for JLPT N4 this year, and the looming Contract Season X in the background, alongside all the anime I have to finish this season, and the gacha games... I feel like it's irresponsible for me to do.

But it's just 200 words. It's not a lot. I can do it in one sitting, literally. I can even have fun with it. Imagine a story that progresses only every 200 words. I can't even begin to imagine how infuriating the cliffhanger would be.
