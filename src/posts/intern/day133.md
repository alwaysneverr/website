I have the weapon in my hand, discreetly taken from my mom's room.

I feel the tip of it gliding over my skin, above my vein. All I needed is to exert a little bit of force, and I will see red.

Yet... once again, I find myself unable to do it. I just kept running it over my skin, my mind is awashed with thoughts of despair.

I am about to fail at the finishing line. I have done something worse than what I did last semester; I've gone no contact until the very last day this time.

The professor surely won't take this well, this time. Why do I push things so far every single time?

Why can't I just finish things well before the deadline like any other normal person?

I can feel the cold steel scrape the epidermis of my arms, leaving tiny lines, but not enough to leave marks.

This has gone so bad that even my parents began to notice how low energy I have become. They probably would have never noticed if I keep up appearance... or maybe they have, but I never knew myself.
