Well... that's all. To be fair, this project should have ended 29 days ago, when I finally took matter into my own hands and started not going to work as a way to resign.

But I kept it going for another month, hoping to maybe continue it into this new job that I had interviewed for.

I didn't make it through.

Unfortunately, good vibe alone is not enough, I suppose. I do have a feeling deep down that I wouldn't make it, but perhaps the words of my parents, brimming with hopefulness, swayed me a little bit into hoping that I would.

Unrelated to all of this, I have progressed really quickly into reading the *Ascendance of a Bookworm* Light Novel series, almost in a competition with one other person who started way later than me, but has since caught up to me. It's crazy how much competition can spur me to do the wildest thing... and coincidentally, some of the things that came up in the series deals exactly about how to manipulate children into trying their best by playing up their competitive spirit!

This light novel series is pretty good.

I don't know how I should end this blog post, honestly. At the beginning of this year, I never would have guessed that my life would be like this. I had more hope that this would be MY year, a year where I can become a proper adult.

In a way, I did became an adult. I'm just really lacking in a lot of aspect that I could not believe that I am.

See you in the 200 words project. That would be the only blog project I'll be working on for now.
