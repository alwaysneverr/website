Twelve days I have abandoned my position. The text came by the first few days, but then...

Silence.

Is this what I should have done months ago? Is this the correct way to end things?

I know deep down that it is not, but I don't know if there is another opportunity after this. 

I let myself get taken into the deep end, the waters of apathy is no longer choking me.

The waters are now like air to me. I breathe it. I let myself get lost in its emptiness. The void helps me to commit unsociable acts like this.

I'm burning the bridge. My passion before has now burnt out to work overdrive in keeping me sane. 

I burn everything.

I know these lies would catch up to me, but I have a feeling that most people are too busy caring about their own problems to care. They will know what I did, but at that point, there is nothing that they can do.


-----

The world itself is burning right now. I can smell the sulfur in the air. There really *was* burning happening around me, but it has since subsided. The chaos might have remained in other parts of the country, but not in this city.

-----

My mom keep reminding me of her own mortality, and she worries that I (and my sister) will not survive whenever she's gone. She is worried that we will not be able to help tend to our father when she's gone.

She is mostly right. I don't intend to burn for him, but she seems to take it that we're incompetent instead of unwilling.

Perhaps this is just the darker side of me that has already planned to abscond from his duties as the children of his parents, but the future I envision once my mom is gone is grim. My mom was right to worry, but she is not worried enough.

She cautions us against her own sister, our aunt. At the very least, it seems she recognizes that we will have to have a conversation about her. Between me and my sister. More caution about sibling fallout because of inheritance.

I just know that there will be issues about it with my sister. I'm not the kind that'll fight about it. If she wants, she can take. I just don't want her to do it because of a third party influencing her. If she is adamant about it, the money should always go to HER, and HER alone. I will fight it if I sense that she is doing it because of someone else.

Our family will burn itself to the ground once my mother left us, though. I don't see a world where my father would survive that long. My mother said he would probably remarry after she is gone, because he can't actually live without someone taking care of him... and she doesn't like to think about what our new stepmom will do to us.

Her imagination is scarier than the darkest imagination I can muster. Perhaps she IS worried enough.

-----

All of this reminded me that I'm going to be a certain age next year. I am running out of time, and I seem to not be able to be my own person yet.

I'm not mature.

Life hasn't gotten to me yet.

I cling to my own childishness as if it is my lifeline, worried that I will fall and hurt myself without it.

My soul screams at me to change yet my subconscious resisted.
