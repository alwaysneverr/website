Over the past week, I've been quite literally enraptured by this game mode, ever since the release of its sixth iteration, Sui's Garden of Grotesqueries. My obsession with this gamemode came directly because of its welfare (read: free) character that you can get by playing through the game mode, Raidian. She went viral when the mode was first released a few months ago... because she's a mommy character with four hands. Hella cute character, VERY STRONG IN THE GAME MODE ITSELF.

This obsession with the game mode came because I needed something to distract me from the current predicament I have in my life. Playing this game quite literally made those hours that would have made me depressed to the point of no return, flew by. I ended up playing until I was stuck on difficulty 5 (Ascension 5 if you are more familiar with Slay the Spire, which I also played recently!).

And I didn't tweet about this one, but I've been playing Integrated Strategies starting from earlier this month, where I managed to get (finally) Ending 2 and 3 from the fourth iteration of the game mode, something that has came out for more than a year now... but I never properly managed to get it since I thought it was *so* hard. (I was just dumb, and watched a YouTube video about how to manage them).
