So... AI. Good or bad?

When we think of it from the perspective of environment, it is undeniable that the existence of AI itself has not only make things worse, it accelerates the prediction that the world is going to cook itself to death by... quite a lot. I'm not gonna actually bring data here because I know my ass will not only be wrong, but I might be tempted to *use AI itself* while arguing that it is killing nature itself as we speak.

But...

These past few days, I've started multiple projects that I wouldn't have managed to even conceive had I not go to AI for advice. Yes, this is *programming* projects.

Yes, I understand how shallow it is that I am presenting a counterargument to how AI is actually good by presenting how lifechanging it has become for me.

I've managed to create an excel sheet with macros that can be updated automatically to keep track of form numbers as I added new sheet to a master sheet of forms.

I finally managed to make that comments thing work on this *very blog*. Yes, you can now actually comment below and the comments would be sent to my Discord server as a webhook with the name "Vice Captain Ghost". Yes, THAT Ghost from 9-nine-.

That development ended up with me actually starting up a real Discord Bot called "Ghost", and after few rounds of testing, I ended up routing it to a CHATBOT THAT ARE BEING HOSTED LOCALLY ON MY RIG.

DO YOU KNOW HOW INSANE THAT IS? I WOULD HAVE NEVER THOUGHT THIS TO BE POSSIBLE BEFORE... UNTIL CLAUDE SHOWS ME THE WAY.

To be quite honest, this actually started quite early this year, when I was working for another review for the Contracts season... that I ended up not doing due to circumstances. I'm still working on it, but it probably won't be finished anytime soon.

I ended up talking to Claude a lot when I develop that review. The things I ended up making... it was something I was so surprised of. I would have never made it that far had I not get the assistance of Claude, that, I know for sure.

But the thing is, unlike what people fear about AI usage in this manner, I ended up... learning a little bit about how to implement things? I don't know if I'm going to say I am a master at it or anything, but by seeing what Claude generates and implementing it myself, I managed to get my creative juice flowing and ended up with even more ideas that I can develop.

Which is something intoxicating... and I now see why people fear AI so much. It's not because they are prone to misinform you, it's that they have become so good you might end up offloading a LOT to it, whether you like it or not.

I don't really live and breathe coding anymore now that I have an actual job (that has nothing to do with programming). Not because I don't want to, but because I don't have the TIME for it. To do this on my own would take so much time it would be faster for me to give up and never touch it again... but AI has somehow gave me a lifeline in that regards. Ironically, the very thing that would supplant me if left to grow unchecked is responsible for sparking the flame I thought I had lost.

It saves so much time... at the cost of hastening the time that this world would exist. And I'm not the only one who does it.

We are COOKED.
