*This is originally a thread of tweets.*

I think 2025 is the year that made me realize that I am a failure.

Of a student.
Of a friend.
Of a boyfriend.
Of a son.
and of a person.

There has not been a year that I've lived in that encapsulates this feeling of being a failure more than this year.

I debated writing this, as I felt like I was being a bit overdramatic. I DID graduate this year from my college. I STILL have friends. I... well, I'm single now, so it's not like I'm being dramatic here, since I did end it myself. I AM helping my parents in any way I can.

But I am running away from what REALLY happened. My degrees are something I'm not passionate about. I ended up being really shitty to my friends this year by ceasing all contact without explanation. I ended things with my girlfriend this year due to my feelings of inadequacy.

And this year, I really feel the weight of being an unemployed son who is too old. I am burdening them with my lack of plans for the future.

I have not carved out my own path, and so I am still piggybacking them in some ways, dragging them down as they slowly come to their own finish line.

Nothing makes you feel like a failure more than seeing your parents die before they can see you succeed without them. At the start of this year, it became apparent that I would have the (dis)honor of making that hypothetical situation real.

All of that makes me realize, genuinely, that I feel like I'm nothing. As I lie here on a sofa beside my father's hospital bed, right before the end of this crappy year, I can't help but feel like I'm a failure of a person.

I would have loved to say that, now that I got it all out in writing, I can start to take the next step forward, instead of ruminating on the worst year of my life.

But I can't even begin to hope anymore.

This year made me realize that all those years ago, all that despair I felt while I was living alone, away from my parents, having to face my issues alone, all of that, is not what true despair really is.

True despair is when you can't even wake up in the morning easily anymore.

True despair is when you fear opening your eyes to wake up to reality, but there is not even a dreamland for you to escape to when you are asleep.

This year made me doubt that I can become a functioning member of society. 

I never had this fear before. 

A younger version of me genuinely thought that by the time I'm at my current age, I should have probably figured it out.

I have not.

If you ask me what I see myself becoming in the next five years, I can't even begin to imagine. It's not like I can answer before, but this time, I really can't see any future at all.

So I guess, before the year really ended, I needed to get this out.

Because this year was the year when I started to not really feel like writing my thoughts down properly, and I guess you can say this is my attempt to rectify that.

I don't dare to hope that next year will be better.

All I wish for is that things will not be worse than this. I don't know if I have enough energy to keep on trying anymore.
