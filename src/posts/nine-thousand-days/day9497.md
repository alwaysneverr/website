The first hour of being twenty-six, I was playing Arknights: Endfield.

The second and third hour of being twenty-six, I might be sleeping, or still playing Arknights: Endfield.

The fourth hour, the fifth, the sixth, and the seventh hour of being twenty-five, I was sleeping. (Surely.)

The seventh to- okay, no.

I can't anymore. I didn't remember this day, as much as I would like to. All I remember is:

- I was out with my family eating ramens that tasted too fishy for our taste
- I was feeling even more fucked up because I graduated already and still has no job
- I might be working on my Persona 5 Royal review (a massive website page)
- I was doomscrolling
- I was locked in on Endfield and playing through the main quest
- A high school friend just sent a wedding invitation and I was researching the path to their wedding spot
- In case I wasn't being clear, I was *doomscrolling*
