In the drowned silence,  
you let yourself lay.  
The dark waters around you,  
brought you down,  
and you closed your eyes.  
You can't see the light.  
It's ever fading, losing its brightness.  
And you give in, to the eternal and enveloping darkness.  

It's okay to give in,  
it's okay to let yourself go,  
but it's not okay to drown in it.  
But in this depth of void, you float, and float.  
Weightless, with no hope to tie you to reality,  
not even a semblance of miracle to grasp on.  

Miracle?  
The truth that hides beneath that gaping hole in your heart?  
There was nothing.  
There was nothing.  
There was... something,  
but it fades away like a fleeting memory of a dream after you wake up.  

In the darkness, you dream of your freedom.  
In the abyss of sleep, you discard your weights and dream of flying.  
In the memory that can never be, you see the clouds and exist with them.  
In the... in the... in the...  

The imagination runs wild,  
and the dreamer weaves his own dream.  
And in the drowned silence,  
the dreamer dream of his victory.
