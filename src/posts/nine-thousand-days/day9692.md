So... the time for this blog to end has come.

Okay, this is not that dramatic. I just mean that it is time for this iteration of blog to stop.

As it is right now, I've spent years making blogs on this site already, to exactly zero people reading (except a few that I forcefeed links to this site in my poor attempt to stroke my own ego and make them read it). I've learned a lot trying to manage this site, and it is quite literally a dream come true to see my writing being organized into multiple pages of my *own* blog site, that I maintain on my own, without anyone doing it for me.

For that I am eternally, and forever, proud of myself for.

But it's time for this cycle of creation to end.

In this form, at least.

I'm going to partially relegate the function of this website to a planned unified website, that contains everything from this blog, to my story writing (that are more complete and less embarassing than the ones that already existed here under the "Story" tag)...

To the crown jewel of all my creations. My magnum opuses (or *magna opera*), you might say.

**The reviews.**

My highly stylized reviews made for the Contracts events, that I have now abandoned due to my increasing lack of time (and my contractual default of the last season I joined, *Season X*). A lot of these I am particularly proud of, and I wanted to show off to everyone in the world to see...

All of this will be hosted on a unified website that I have been working to replace the static HTML site that I unfortunately have to take the plug off late last year. This site will also use Eleventy framework, which makes migration a cinch since I basically just need to copy the entire folder containing my markdown files to the new website's folder.

But here's the kicker: I have SEVEN HUNDRED SEVENTY FIVE (counting this post) BLOG POSTS ON THIS BLOG.

SEVEN HUNDRED SEVENTY FIVE. 775.

That is a LOT of posts to be served on my new site. And some of these posts has a VERY GOOD CHANCE to reveal my real identity.

There's a few train of thoughts I had regarding this. One, the point about potential doxxing material is honestly kinda moot, because of this blog's very existence. It's already out on the internet for everyone to see. 

Counterpoint, however; this blog is not actually visible at all when you search on Google.

I don't know why, exactly, but I have a feeling it's because there's a robots.txt setting somewhere here that has blocked the entire site from being indexed by Google Search. I think.

Two, the sheer amount of blogposts are not actually a big concern, since I've already planned for my site to be able to host ALL of these posts. And from my own testing, the website can handle the bulk of it just fine (my 2020 project and my 2024 project).

Well, it can at least generate the texts and embeds. Images are still broken. It's a completely different layout, and different vibes.

To address both of these, I thought I could do something like this; I will port only a *select* amount of posts from this blog to my main site, and continue this site as it is. I'll be able to populate my unified website with well-curated blog posts that don't feel like they're notes at times (looking at you, most of 2020 project), and I will still continue writing blogposts to this site. Perhaps this site will remain as a darker, more secretive place to harbor my thoughts.

In a way, you can actually say that I'm not actually ending things here. Technically speaking, you'd be right.

But my intent remains for me to stop making this my MAIN go-to site to publish my blog posts. It will remain, sure, but it is no longer my priority. The purpose of this site has shifted.

It is still the end of how things works so far, though. The end of an era.

Thank you so much if you have actually read through all of my 775 blogposts and reached this post. Or if you actually keep track of this blog somehow through the RSS feed (yes, I do have a RSS feed). Or if you somehow found yourself from any other place, or from my own shilling XD.

I will see you next time when my site go live.
