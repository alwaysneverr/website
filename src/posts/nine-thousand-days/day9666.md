Hi there.

So... uhh...

A lot of things happened between the beginning of the year until this day. As you can see... six months has passed.

Let's just get on with the big stuff first; my father died. Yeah, may he rest in peace.

I got a job. Yes, an actual job in an actual company with actual salary. Like, non-intern, big boy salary.

I am suddenly taking care of stuff that I only ever thought I would do years down the line. Like, stuff that I know I would have to take care of once I realized my parents are sick.

And... I bought a mini PC.

And a keyboard.

And a concert ticket.

Why does it feel like I'm not... grieving? It felt like I'm suddenly doing things that I wouldn't before instead of properly grieved for a person that is responsible for making sure I can live comfortably.

Why...?

For the past 47 days since he passed away, that's all I can think of whenever I have time to reflect. Am I being a proper kid that laments over the sudden passing of his father?

Or do I seem like an opportunist, doing stuff that I wouldn't do when he lived, now that he is gone?

For fuck's sake, man, I booked a concert ticket to SINGAPORE. ALONE. I would have never done this before. Not when... *he* lived.

Does he left the birdcage door ajar as his final action in life?

I don't know... I don't know.
