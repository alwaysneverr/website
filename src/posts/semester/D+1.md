A Small Station

A lone station deep down the line. Small, compared to most other station, but its architecture is generally made to be a passing station. Passengers waited in the seats, most were regular citizens. A few were actually foreigners. Backpackers, I can only assume. Heat radiated as if there's no canopy over my head; it warped the surroundings like a mirage. Two trains were here, stationary, on standby, waiting for something. Their ambient heat compounded to the hot air that has been sweeping to my body.

In here I make a return trip back to the city, after I made a mistake that costs me time and wasted tickets.

In my train, I supposed to sit and wait until I reached my destination. 5 hour trip, from 7.30 AM. I woke up at 6, and reached the station at 7.10 AM. 

It was a normal day, isn't it? There should be nothing wrong with it.

But I made a major mistake. Two trains, similar to the one that were here, is in the first station, were standing by waiting to depart. One of them was the train I supposed to be boarding. But instead I took the other train.A different train.

Multiple and multiple of calls by the condecteur(*sic*) were made, reaffirming where the train is headed, but I miss it. By the time I reached a few stations I realized my error.

I reached out first to the lady who were carrying a food tray. She said and confirmed where the train headed. Cue embarrassment, but I learned to laugh it off. Treat it like a mistake, and it can be managed.

Then afterwards the lady recommended me to ask for guidance from any officer or the condecteur(*sic*) himself. I managed my emotions, yes, but I am not ready to walk up to an officer who were probably a carriage or more away.

So I waited, and there he was, an officer heading my way. I consulted to him, and he recommended me to take a train back to the first station and buy another ticket for a train heading to my destination.

But first I have to get off at the next station.

I monitored my situation. I have a chance to still make it to my destination today. The condecteur(*sic*) has given me an advice, and I intend to follow it. But, the window of opportunity is closing. 

The schedule for trains are all available online, in an app. This app were the one that I used to buy the now obsolete ticket. The train that was heading back are arriving mere half an hour from now. I was still in motion, the train have not arrived in the next station yet.

And the ticket I need to go to my destination? There's only 3 of them left. Or four, the application seems glitched about that.

In order to actually buy a ticket, if you're buying through an app, you need to pay from outside source. I paid my ticket from a convenience store, and because I can't do that from inside a moving train, I have no choice but to wait until I reached the station to buy it from their own internal seller. 

The train slowed to a stop, and I rushed like I was being chased by debt collector. I zip through the carriage, not caring over the possible looks that the passengers given me.

I jumped through the door and exited the train. I ran to the exit, and when I did, I heard a call.

The officer who helped me reminded me to buy the ticket as soon as I can.

I don't need to be reminded.

I ran.

The seller location were not far, just by the exit. I hurried over and asked for a ticket. She question me, confused. Why am I rushing? I tell her my abbreviated story of mishap, she can only smile a little.

She's kinda cute. Eh, BACK TO THE STORY.

I buy the ticket back first, and then I asked her for another ticket onward.
