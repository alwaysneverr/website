*ATRI* reordered even more sequence from the VN... and adding Nonko as an actual character and potential last villain of the series, which is insane... I love it though. This is still a fresh take of the story that they have on the VN, and I still think it's an overall improvement, which might be an insane thing to say...

-----

*Makeine* last episode is a goofy episode concerning Nukumizu and Anna going on a "date" (with Lemon and Komari in tow so it's not a romantic date) in order to show Anna's friend that she's no longer single, and so she won't get approached or asked about it again. Compared to the other episode, you can clearly see that this is just a filler episode, but it's chockfull of great animation nonetheless, including some horror scenes and some Kaju goodies too... man, this made me want to read the LN, honestly.

If their goal for this to get people to read the LN, they definitely succeed. Wait... I think I've said this before for another series that adapts from a Light Novel series...
