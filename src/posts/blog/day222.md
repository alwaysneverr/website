*Gimai Seikatsu* gets a bit interesting as we get the super shiny Maaya, who basically become the only character that shows an emotion more than ambivalence in this anime... like, everything is so chill, and they keep focusing on the chill vibes, but when things get dramatic, they depict it in a way that left me a bit confused, focusing on style instead of substance, in my opinion. I'm not gonna say this is a bad show, though, just something that rely a bit too much on chill vibes, but this could be influenced by the actual LN itself, and if that's true, then there's no helping how the adaptation goes.

Might have to add this LN to the list of my ever growing LNs PTR...
