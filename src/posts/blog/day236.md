*Gimai Seikatsu* continues as we see flashback featuring Saki and her mother. Yuuta, who wants to push Saki to get out more due to her mom's worry, clashes with Saki about Maaya and her invitation to go to the pool. Yuuta is worried about Saki and called her out, knowing that she likely wants to go to the pool but she still refuses to go, and saying that she need to relax at some point. Saki, however, bursts out saying she can't relax, which might be related to her wish to grow up.

Afterwards, Saki silently gave Yuuta Maaya's contact information before leaving the room. We can see then that she has trouble sleeping, and she woke up too late to serve breakfast for Yuuta's father. Here, though, we get to see Yuuta assert himself to Saki, and make breakfast. Saki so far has been pushing herself, including cooking for everyone, but Yuuta wants to give back to her in some form, related to him wanting her to "relax".

Seeing his effort warms Saki's heart, and she relents, saying that they can go to the pool together. On their way to the pool (I'm assuming), we see Saki asking Yuuta about his relationship with Shiori, assuming they were dating, but Yuuta denied it. He then wondered why she think that way. Saki cannot answer, and instead start pointing out Shiori's good points, indirectly promoting her, and stops herself before she can say anything further.

After all these episodes, I honestly started feeling like SOMEONE in the cast has feelings. And no, if you are going to say "Yuuta has feelings, he got angry at Saki since she didn't watch the road", WE DON'T GET TO SEE THAT PROPERLY. It's all adapted in such artsy ways, without voice acting, and just plain implied that I was confused as fuck when that happened.

Anyway, we also see a mysterious girl this episode, and also the tease at the end of the episode might lead to a flashback involving Yuuta's father, which is interesting.
