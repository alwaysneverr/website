<summary>Animanga segment (Gimai Seikatsu, ATRI, Makeine, Dark Gathering)</summary>

*Gimai Seikatsu* this week is a heartbreaking episode where Saki realized that she has fallen in love with Yuuta, but decides to lock her feeling away as she took on the traditional haircut of the heartbroken: cutting it short. This time, there is a justification, since Yuuta said that he actually preferred a long haired girl, which means she is attempting to move on by becoming someone that Yuuta would not even look at, becoming his sister, and even calling him... onii-chan.

I'm just...

Let me just lay down I'm feeling kinda down right now, maybe... maybe ATRI would help me feel better-

-----

*ATRI* anime... doesn't get fuckin' better as we finally get to THAT point in the VN, where Natsuki realized that ATRI has been faking her "love" for him.

God, this week has been quite the despairing line of episodes, I can only hope Makeine can lift me up-

-----

*Makeine*... actually gets a bit funny as the lit club prepares for their school festival, introducing new characters like Tiara and the council president/villainess ahh character. Thank *god*, I need a breather after those two episodes before this.

-----

In *Dark Gathering*'s next few chapters, we get to see the nature of the affliction that Keitaro get from his incident years ago (that also affects Eiko, making them tied together in some fashion), and Yayoi teaching Keitaro about how to keep himself safe from these dreaded malicious spirits... in some scary manner, that is. I mean... using spirits she has captured to take the hit by mixing the doll they're trapped in with body parts? Like... holy shit!

We also get to see even more scary yandere stuff from Eiko... girl are you possessed to do this or not? If you're NOT, that makes it EVEN SCARIER.

The story goes through some chapters where they fight what is basically villains-of-the-chapter, like a doll spirit that tries to choke Keitaro with her hair, an old Goddess spirit that eats children who Yayoi fights, a malicious spirit who burns alive those who watches the tape they made when they're still alive, a spirit that possess people to help them kill people by suicide, and *spirits* (plural) that dwell in a bridge where they all died.

That last spirit took two chapters to be taken down, and the stakes for that one is higher since they managed to possess Eiko, and Yayoi has to do some clever maneuver to get them to stop... The shounen aspect blended with supernatural is a new thing for me, but this kind of writing makes me remember that this is probably marketed AS shounen, and they're rocking it for sure! The takedowns for all the other spirits too were AWESOME and CLEVER. I can't wait to see how this develops...

Oh, speaking of which, after that last incident, Keitaro and Eiko is a thing now... but Eiko has to be all weird about it. WHAT IS WRONG WITH YOU? WHY ARE YOU LIKE THISSSSS???

These chapters also introduces some outtakes, which actually made me smile a bit as a way to lighten things up after each chapters, and I appreciate that a lot. Reading the afterword also made me realize that the author, Kondou Kenichi, doesn't usually write horror... but holy shirtballs his drawing is REALLY scary. I mean, if this is his first try, you are doing quite well, sir.

<summary>NIKKE x Evangelion</summary>
I actually started reading the NIKKE x Evangelion collab story... just like when I read the Re:Zero collab... and the story is pretty interesting. The event before this already warm us up to the students of the MMR Vocational School (I only read Ein's story, as I completely missed Zwei's story event which was a bit of an oopsie, and I wasn't there for Rei's story), and their interaction with the Evangelion characters are pretty funny. The hot-headed Asuka with the cool Zwei, and the opposite cool-headed Rei Ayanami (oh yeah, there's two Rei in this team-up...) with the semi hot-headed gyaru Ein... alongside Rei the Nikke with Mari, where Mari just keep teasing her.

To be honest, I didn't expect the story to be so dire, as the threat level for the Gatekeeper for this collaboration is quite high, as the gatekeeper itself attacks the Ark directly. I thought these type of story is reserved for the main story antagonists, with the Rapture and their Queen... but it seems like they're cooking with this one. Comparing this to Re:Zero collab seems unfair, because the Re:Zero collab is about maids and food competitions...
