*Wind and Truth* preview chapters continues as Kaladin goes on a goodbye tour... really making me dread for the worse... BRANDONNNNNNN...

We also get Shallan, fresh from her finally accepting she has multiple personality, and now can finally conduct herself as a Knight Radiant properly in the Shadesmar. She is a long way from panicking when she first arrived in Shadesmar, like, four books ago in *Words of Radiance*... if only Jasnah can see her pupil becoming badass, slowly inching closer to her own badass level...

-----

Started *Ascendance of a Bookworm*, and right off the bat, we get a protagonist that is... quite the *brat*. Urano is a book geek (and I mean, an absolute book geek) that lost her life in an earthquake as her mountain of books she owns literally killed her, and she gets reborn as a 5 year-old Myne, a brat that are prone to be sick... in a world WITHOUT BOOKS. The horror...

Anyway, we get to be introduced to Myne's family, including Gunther, a dad that tries his best and I appreciate him, Effa, a strict but lovely mother, and her older sister Tuuli, who is very reliable and has cute interactions with her... I finished half of the volume and finding myself in love with the family, and other characters beside them like Lutz and Otto (who is somehow unreliably awesome), and can't wait to see how Myne's endeavor in making paper going to become!
