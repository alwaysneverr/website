Skribbl.io time!

Scraped through the first round... these bois are not playing around, I can't get an edge with them.

Their guessing game is insane! I wish at least one of our server rep gets in to the final lobby, at least, but they paired together me and my friend from the same server for the second round...

I already feel like not playing when that happens, but I still continue sweating my ass off... but I failed. I needed to get top 2, but I ended up scraping to fourth, which is good enough to get to a consolation lobby... a lobby for dweebs who barely managed to be better than the finals big bois. (Okay, some of these players are good though)

And even worse, the consolation lobby was supposed to be there in case the other teams has too many duplicate players on the final lobby, but the players are evenly balanced for all the servers (and alliances, no we will not talk about that). Anyway, apparently to entice people to continue playing in the consolation lobby, the winner of the lobby would be entered into a 1 month Nitro raffle.

I was kinda miffed at this point from missing out points, but I still want to play more, even though I should be sleeping soon cause tomorrow I have a big trip, but I continued playing... and I won.

I won... on this lobby. Not the previous lobby where it mattered, or even the first round where that would give me some ego boost at least. This one.

Sigh... well, at least the event IS fun, playing Skribbl.io is always a treat even if I'm not winning. Some of the things these players write to describe their words are TOO funny XD

And I even played an "after-party" game after all the event thing is over against the champion herself... she's both good at drawing and guessing, like, lightning fast, so I don't feel too bad since I know I'll be beat anyway if I made it to the finals lobby.
