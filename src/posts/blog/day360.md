On Christmas today, a miracle happened.

Five people across the globe get together to watch the official release of the movie *Rascal Does Not Dream of a Knapsack Kid*.

It was like a dream. Groupwatch for groups over three people are unthinkable, because of the timing issues between the participants. But on this Christmas Day, we all seem to agree on spending at least an hour on watching this movie.

It was magical. Maybe it's a bit cringe for me to put this on a pedestal but I do believe that this is a monumental feat.
