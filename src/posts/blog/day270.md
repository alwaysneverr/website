*Wind and Truth* is somehow a little steamy this week. It was released a few days ago but I've forgotten to read it...

But first of all, let me just say, Brandon did one thing with his chapter this week that I immensely enjoyed, which is a repeated phrase that ties different POV together. In this case, it was "Not Asleep".

This chapter involves different POVs of monarchs in the alliance that has trouble sleeping because of the incoming deadline and the complications behind that deadline, including the surprise attack by their enemies on multiple fronts.

You might wonder at this point, what do I mean by steamy, then?

Well, for people to not be asleep, in certain cases they are doing... something. And boy oh *boy*, Brandon is not even implying anymore at this point. They are *bonking*, like, maybe not an explicit description of them doing it, yeah, but he actually say "sex" at one point. This is unprecedented ever since... probably *Warbreaker*? And we know that *that* book in particular is written during his honeymoon.

Parts of this book was written when he was vacationing in Hawaii...

But yeah... he just goes for it now, we got Jasnah and Wit doing it, Queen Fen and her Prince Consort (and they're OLD old), and SHALLAN AND ADOLIN.

YEAH, THEM TOO. AND YOU KNOW WHAT'S GREAT ABOUT IT? THEY'RE HAVING *SHOWER SEX*. That's why I said *steamy*.

And even better than this, Showers are only recently discovered because Navani and her bond to the Sibling (we get some of her POV this week too, involving how she can actually choose not to sleep if she wants due to the bond) unlocking the full potential of Urithiru... this city is now a modern city in a medieval times...

And it is possible that Shallan and Adolin is one of the first few person who had sex while in shower. The thought of them being the first just made me chuckle...

Anyway, yeah, there's some more POV about Yanagawn unable to even sleep without someone watching him (Azir culture is reaaaaaal weird) and some more Adolin angst about his father, promising us a very tearful resolution for this later...

Oh, and uhh... CULTIVATION HERSELF IS HERE. IN-PERSON. JUST... *STROLLS BY* TO MEET DALINAR.

This is already rivalling *The Way of Kings* and *Words of Radiance*. I cannot begin to wait for the full book to be released...
