The last batch of *Princess Principal* starts with a flashback to Ange and Dorothy's past in a woman-only spy training facility (like the Red Room) called "The Farm", and their classmates who they have to investigate because of her potential to be a double agent...

Well... the horrific reality of spies are again on full display here, but at this point I'm more or less accepted that this gonna keep happening. My only worry is now about the main characters, but they do have a movie as a sequel to this... a six part movie, at that. But... the last few seconds of the episode brought that worry to the forefront, as Control changes leadership, and their new leader demands them to assassinate Charlotte...

...and of course, this sudden development comes with massive changes to status quo, all in order to isolate Ange and Charlotte from possible shenanigans. Dorothy is reassigned, Chise is pulled back by her vassal, and Beatrice is just... missing. Suspicious.

Anyway, yeah, the second-to-last episode quickly ramps up the tension as Control's leadership is handled by an army general, who hasten the plot to go to war. Ange and Charlotte has a disagreement on what to do, as Ange wants to run, but Charlotte still insist on staying, so she locked Ange into a ship, and pretended to be her *pretending* to be Princess Charlotte, running with Operation Changeling for real.

Spies, man...

The last episode is quite action packed, and as I suspected, Beatrice was in cahoots with the hidden element to help Ange, which turns out to be Dorothy! (who approached her in the previous episode then....? Seven?)

The schemes of Zelda, who also has her own C-ball which is already rare, is thwarted as Ange and Chise managed to save her, while Dorothy and Beatrice (mostly Dorothy) creates havoc on the event the schemes are supposed to be taking place, thereby managing to save the Queen and stop the country from destabilizing...

And once the episode ends, they all live in Casablanca now, taking their well-earned vacation from all the drama they've been having... wait, why is Chise with them? I mean... awww... but also damn, she's a long way from home... 

I need to watch the movie for this. This is definitely not the end of the series. I can't even begin to imagine if I watched this as it airs back in 2017. I have to wait YEARS until the sequel is properly made, and it's a SIX-PART MOVIE, with only THREE released so far... like, DAMN.

Anyway, I do have to move on, though. I'm still not sure which series I might watch next, but I might keep Chuunibyou as last because I think I might enjoy it too much... so I'll take either Saiki K or Aikatsu Planet, leaning on the latter.
