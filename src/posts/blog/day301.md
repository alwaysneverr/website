I'm in a bit of yet another *Worlds* binge, just like last year. It's the semis. T1 is facing off against GEN.G, something that they managed to avoid last year because GEN.G choked their quarter-finals against a LPL team... this year, they *almost* choked yet another quarter-finals, but against a LCS team in a 3-2 score. You know how fucked up that is?

LCS teams hasn't made semis in years, and they were really close to getting in this year. And LCS is a meme region at this point. We're not talking about LEC here, which would be great since they also haven't made semis in a while, but at least some of them have made it to finals, and ARE a force to be reckoned with. LCS? They have never won *a single game* against an LCK team... and ALSO the LPL team.

And now that streak is broken as they won two games off GEN.G. Insane, I tell you...

Anyway, this weekend, T1 vs GEN.G is something people have been interested to see. Because their rivalry is famous in their own region. T1 haven't won a series off GEN.G since last year's MSI 2023. They haven't even won finals in any event since 2022, and it's been told that T1's kryptonite is GEN.G...

Well, seems like that is now a myth as T1 broke GEN.G's back... decisively.
