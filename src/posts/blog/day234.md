*Spice & Wolf* starts a new arc (a brand new one that the old anime did not adapt, I'm excited to see where this goes) as Lawrence and Holo enters an unhospitable town, meet a farmer that turns out has potentially illicit relationship with the sole priestess of the Church? I don't know where this is going, but if what Holo said is true (about how the relationship of the previously mentioned couple is a mirror to their own), this can lead to a really good seasonal ending... Season 2 announcement should be coming soon... I hope...

*Bye Bye Earth* ends their Katakombe arc with a massive fight where... a lot of people died, and a standoff between Tiziano, Adonis, and Belle (jesus fucking christ Adonis just used his blink dog thingy as a way to stand up the endless space they're trapped in, that was so fucking cool). A lot of things happened, things I still really don't know how to explain, but to make it simple, Tiziano is corrupted by a sword, that might have connections to Gods, and Tiziano and Adonis is implied to have something going on before this. Anyway, everything's cleared up now, and we will see the pink haired girl we've been seeing in the OP next week... 4 more episodes, what would they bring with these...
