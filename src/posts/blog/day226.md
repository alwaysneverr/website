This is a bit unfortunate because I wish to finish ATRI first at least before even starting this highly rated VN, but I'm progressing along well above, and I think it's possible for me to finish them really soon, which would make me able to focus on *White Album 2* completely.

So far, the story has been slow, and the art is just as I expected, a bit dated, but not so much that it is distracting. The story so far concerns the member of Light Music Club, and super honor student who helps people everywhere, Haruki, as he has to find new members for his club since everyone else excluding his friend and the club leader Takeya has fled the club because of manipulations of a specific member... 

Through some events and chance encounter, he figured out that the most beautiful girl in his grade (winning the school beauty pageant two times, and potentially the third time in her third year) Setsuna Ogiso, can sing really well, along with the secret that she is not as well put together as she projected to everyone at school.
