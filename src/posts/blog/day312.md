Decided to start my journey into the second season of *Chuunibyou demo Koi ga Shitai!* today... and I can definitely tell the vibes are WAAAAAYYY OFF. Like, it might've been a few weeks ever since I finished the first season, but I can definitely tell that there's something missing in this season. I can't really tell you what it is, but let's just say the tropes are still there, it's still funny... but it felt like the characters are more goofy? Especially Rikka, which at some point I just keep looking and wonder... who is this girl? I mean, sure, this might be who she is actually as she fully embrace her chuuni self with her own self, but... I don't know. I don't think it's right to say this is a mischaracterization just yet. I haven't even read the original novel, and apparently the new character that's coming this season is someone we do see on the original novel, so maybe the weird vibes going to pass....
