*Princess Principal* has a really... *inconsequential* episode? out of all the episode with high tension and consequences so far... Episode 7, or the laundry episode, exhibits the kind of mix between fun and job, but leaning towards the fun, as the team refurbished a laundry mill in order to do their job and find a psychotic poison gas villain hiding in the ranks of the Kingdom's army. Of course, it's only possible because the laundry mill takes on the job of washing their clothes, which they use as an opportunity to test the clothes for chemical reactions.

Without knowing how it ends, I was fearing the worst to happen, including how all these kids working in the laundry mill might die.. but thankfully that did not happen... and the ownership of the mill is even transferred to one of the girls working there initially...

Well, the next episode, episode 8 brought back the dark vibes as we finally learn why this story is inspired by *The Prince and The Pauper*, and we get told of how Princess Charlotte and the Pickpocket Ange was originally switching roles frequently during their childhood, but was unable to switch back one day because of a rebellion. The current Ange (the old Princess Charlotte) laments on how the old Ange (the current Princess Charlotte) works really hard to survive and assuming the role of princess, and the pressure she must have felt hiding her identity amongst the paranoid nobles who would have executed her when they found out...

Honestly, that situation actually brought a bit of fear and sadness to my heart, because I can't even begin to imagine having to be forced onto such role to play for *years* with my head on the line. At the end, though, (current) Ange admits that (current) Charlotte is more of a princess than she ever was, as her effort has lead her to become a formidable princess.

*ATRI*'s last episode... finally gets to the true ending. I'll say this, animation-wise it is no slouch, and also they added a ton of stuff in like the DRESS FOR ATRI?? LIKE-

As a VN reader this is a welcome change, but the way the ending is being adapted... does fall of short just slightly behind the VN. I'd say it's more of an awkward ending that does get me a bit but compared to the VN? It's slightly lesser.

Either way, they don't change the ending that much, only that Minamo didn't get to marry Natsuki this time, which I'm indifferent about... a bit sad, but Minamo seems to be happy in this adaptation too, so I'll take it. I also noticed that by designing Yoko (that friend who do have her own VA but no design) and Ririka's friends (the one that was literally nameless at some point in the VN) kinda helps in making the show has more life to it... but it also make them lack focus on these characters. I didn't feel the same kind of closeness as I've had with the group in the VN...

Either way, this is a fair 7/10 adaptation. Not to shabby, would I say it's peak? Just for ATRI alone, I'm willing to say that, because she is pretty much the pillar for this anime that has her name on it, which is somehow a tall order for another character in another anime that has the same name this season...
