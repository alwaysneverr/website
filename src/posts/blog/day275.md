Finished the Introductory Chapter for *White Album 2*... what can I say except I'm... slightly frustrated? Not really because of the quality of the story, of course. If we're talking about that, I can confidently say that they're cooking with this, especially with the circular narrative and how everything ties itself up nicely at the end, but the character themselves just frustrate me to no ends... just like how characters in DomeKano frustrate me sometimes too! This is a feeling that I personally don't mind, but it kinda hinders me on really talking about the characters sometimes, because their mistake is something that is so complex, I can't fully blame them nor excuse them, and it's just frustrating to think about...

In the situation between Haruki, Kazusa, and Setsuna, there's really no point, and no person, can be fully blamed for doing whatever they did at the end... from betraying the person they chose, taking away someone's lover to themselves, or knowingly forces a person to make a choice that benefits them...

Well, let's just say I need the next part of the story (featuring three new girls... THREE!) to be about moving on from this and Haruki finding himself, even if I hate him a ton...
