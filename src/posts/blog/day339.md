I dreamed a story scenario today... but it's pretty weird.

A man gets stuck in another dimension through unknown means, and meet up with its denizen. Specifically a green haired girl called Vinny. (This girl's name and her green hair is something that actually sticks out from my memory of this dream. I have NO IDEA why.) He worked together with the denizens of this world (a ruined world, I think), and managed to make a machine that could send him back to his original world.

Oh, and by the way, this man is based on Goku. (Yes, just roll with it.) And so there's some details about how he could just fly away to space but it seems like this world is a parallel world of his own, and he has no chance to ever finding his own world by travelling through space. I think my mind starts to think of that plot from the novel Dark Matter, and how improbable that is.

Wait... actually the plot of this story is basically Dark Matter without that one specific plot point....

Anyway, yeah, as the man about to enter the machine and leave, he realized he has fallen in love with Vinny, and even though he tried his hardest to deny it, because he has a wife on his home dimension, he cannot, and he left with a goodbye kiss to her.

(My friend pointed out once again that I have made a somehow NTR-like situation. Welp, what can you do...)

Anyway, he returns to find out his wife has died. (Yes, this wife is based on Chi-Chi. Again, just roll with it.) Specifically, died trying to find out where he goes, and now he has to grapple with that fact... and how he has a child (maybe son?) that has survived all this time without a father.

That's all I remember from the dream, I think there's no continuation anyway from that part. It's been a while since I have a dream this vivid that it leaves an impression to me hours later. Especially the girl for some reason...

My mom's words echoes a lot in my mind recently.

"Don't let yourself get used by this friend of yours!"

I feel like I haven't truly let go of that statement. I might be a bit too dramatic about this, letting just this one single sentence hanging around my brain and getting worked up over a potentially manipulative words, but...

I guess that's why they call it manipulative, because I'm *swayed* by them regardless, even if I want to believe it's not true.

The best manipulative words always seem to be derived from true statements, and in this case, my mom is not entirely wrong. I *am* getting used by my friends as someone with a transportation to carry them to multiple places. But I think it's wrong to say that in those way. This just imply that I'm getting *nothing* out of the interaction, I only give my service and didn't receive anything in return.

Because, like, if my mom can say this about my friends, how about the instances where *she* does the same to me? She can say immediately that we're family and it's my duty as her son... but at times I do feel more like I'm their butler instead of their son. I joked about that once with my mom, who seems perturbed that I even think of our relationship that way.

But the cat is out of the bag. I don't accept that my mom said that while doing pretty much the same thing, with the only difference between each actions derived from our blood ties. It's infuriating how I've catered to all of her whims and all it took is just one time I didn't do exactly as she mentioned and everything I've done for her is thrown to the bin. It's like she doesn't believe in me, like she doesn't trust me. She worries a lot, and even I can't deny that her worries are unfounded, but her worry sometimes felt stifling.

It gets me to think that she doesn't believe that I will survive once she's gone. 

She's pushing for my whole family to watch a movie about dying mom recently, pushing a very obvious message across the whole family about her mortality. I don't know what to feel about it. I'm scared that I won't cry and would make her disappointed.
