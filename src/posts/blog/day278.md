*Princess Principal*... continues to baffle me as they blend in anime girls with a rather depressing setting and plot. I mean, episode 4 brings more tension as we were told that everyone has tenuous relationship with each other due to their "grey" status, including the new member Chise who were there on behalf of the Japan. The episode tries to make us doubt everyone's loyalty, but Princess Charlotte proves to be the heart of the team as her actions shows her clear intention to cooperate.

We also get to see her introduction episode, episode 5, as she involves herself in an assassination plot by a famous killer, that we were led to believe killed her father, but turns out revealed to be her actual father... and we got to watch her kill him by her own hands... yikes man...

Episode 6 is also very dark, as the timeline now finally passes over the first episode (as you can probably guess, the way they're airing this episodes jumps through timelines as an artistic choice, which is pretty good, as far as I can tell), but we get to see Dorothy (partnered with Beatrice) meeting her abusive father in a mission involving morgue. They got me feeling a lot of things, including even a bit of sadness as her father dies trying to secure money for Dorothy by bargaining against a dangerous spy... I... this is just sad... I need to go to sleep.
