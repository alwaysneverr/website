Played *Sunless Skies* again after stopping for a while, on a completely new save (and from Epic Games Launcher as well). I almost died doing this once as I reach 1 HP fighting a Scrive-Spinster, those sons of bitches... but I managed to get through and now explored almost 75% of the *first* map, missing one station and the pathway to Albion and to the London in the sky... man, this game is LONG long...
