*Wind and Truth* preview chapters is finally released, and we get to see something other than the prologue for once (unless you follow his myriad of readings on many events, but I've been keeping myself pure to read these preview chapters instead).

First of all, there is NO Ketek, we just get "Day One" for parts now... this is insane. There's ten days until the contest of champions... so we're gonna get the whole ten days? This has to be one of the most important ten days of Roshar...

And then we get Epigraphs, taken straight from the books *Knights of Wind and Truth*... heh, he managed to put that Ketek theory back in after all, the one I talked about wayyyyy back in [Two Thousand Twenty Words project on day 1](/posts/2020project/2020day1/). 

Also, we get to see Kaladin and Syl... honestly, the way it is written kinda awakens my shipping parts, but I need to contain them. But like.. Syl and Kaladin's interactions felt a lot closer now, not that of a spren and the Radiant Knight, but more... familial, or even more. Perhaps this is because he is close to the Fifth Ideal? Who knows...

But also, I can't help but feel like this chapter is his farewell to his parents, alongside his new brother, Oroden. Man... the death flags...

Also, Dungspren. This is a promise, Brandon. You *better* put this to rest at some point in the series. Do they exist or not? Gahhh you tease master...
