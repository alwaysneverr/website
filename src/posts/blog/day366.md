Hello, 2025 Al here. (Exactly about 51 days after this post's supposed date, or 366+51.)

I didn't manage this project until the very end, because of things I cannot mention but was urgent and very distracting. 2024 Al during this day spent his time playing Arknights (RA2 specifically), losing himself on many rabbit holes like the *House* tv show clips, *Doctor Who* clips, *Peaky Blinders* clips, *The Rookie* clips, the Drake-Kendrick beef, watching LAZARBEAM's videos, working a bit on *Oshi no Ko* wiki (I'm no longer considering actively working on it, because it has ended, and the story's ending is... after a few months, doesn't sit right with me), and ended up sleeping before the midnight even come by, while all of his family is still up.

I must say, I don't really feel the magic of New Year at the time, and I feel like I haven't felt it in a long time...

But still, I want to leave this project with a good feeling, and so I want to write a good end note.

This project has been quite the dream come true... for an older me. For current me, it has become untenable in ways that I cannot fully grasp when I was younger. Most of this lies on me switching to using screenshots to do the heavy lifting of illustrating my day, most likely a screenshot of what Gacha I'm playing and winning at the day, or which anime/visual novel I'm consuming. 

Due to how this website is structured, I have to process the image in order not to overload the server that this website is hosting with too much assets. Even after I scale back the images halfway through the project, the project takes about half a gigabyte of images. *That* means this is no longer a writing project, just a screenshot galore that doesn't represent my thoughts of the day. 

Scaling back the images didn't make this better, as what I put in the pages now felt like I'm describing images or screenshot that I've taken. 

I no longer am open to sharing what I feel. 

I no longer felt like I should *write* about it.

I think I know part of the reason why, and I hope this is now no longer an issue, but it seems like being an adult means you're going to face issues after issues that comes by like an infinite wave, and there's no avoiding it.

This project however does make me proud. If I put my mind to it, I *can* be consistent (even if there were hiccups here and there). This blooms into other things that I want to try, as now I'm trying to learn Japanese in one year by using Wanikani and Anki decks (Kaishi 1.5k). Hopefully I can be consistent in this endeavor as well.
