*Oshi no Ko* episode 11 depicts one of the few stretches of this manga that I'm kinda ambivalent on (Kana date, and the whole talk Akane and Aqua has on the bridge) into one big treat. Kana date is tolerable (and slightly funny at times), and they changed it so that it came earlier than Akane and Aqua talk on the bridge, which contributed to how the ending of this episode works, making the reveal that Hikaru is still alive and well (along with the visual representative of Akane realizing the loophole, and the SLIGHT TEASE ABOUT HER FAKEOUT DEATH, I SEE YOU ANIMATORS) really makes me realize how much the animators have perfectly used the medium to their advantage. They not only bring the manga to life, but bring it in such an artistic way, I can't help but be awed by it...

-----

*Spice & Wolf* gets spicy as the village turns on Holo and Lawrence after it was revealed that someone died after eating a poisonous wheat from Tereo, suspicion laying on them due to how they are the only outsider at the moment in the village. The discovery of the wheat being poisonous has potentially brought the entire village into ruins, as the church is now bringing back all of the wheat to the village and demanding the return of the money they have paid, thereby depriving Tereo from the gains they potentially get after their harvest season. Now, they are potentially penniless, because if the wheat is indeed poisonous, they cannot sell them back because the whole batch of their wheat might be contaminated.

This situation is a bit hard to wrap my head around, especially with the intrigue of this being an Enberch plotting to break the agreement with Tereo made by late Father Franz, but for now, they have no choice but to escape the village, lest they would be the perfect scapegoat for the panicking villager, who would soon see them dead in order to get them to survive, even if the fact is that they are innocent.

And now our couple, along with another couple, Elsa and Evan, is on the run, never to come back to the village, lest their lives will be stripped from them by force...
