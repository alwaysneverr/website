*Imasara desu ga, Osananajimi wo Suki ni Natte Shimaimashita*... is a story that has been seen too many times. A girl finding out that she has fallen in love with her childhood friend. Nothing about this story stood against its contemporaries. There are a few reasons that made me interested in this series, though. First, YOM is illustrating the series. Second, this series is a webcomic adapting chapters of a webnovel that is released simultaneously with the chapter(?), which is an interesting way to do this, since it's usually adaptations of an already existing webnovel. Third, it's childhood friend romance, man. As you can see [a few days ago](/posts/blog/day222/) with *Childhood Friend Complex*, I'm on some childhood friends binge right now.

BUT. There's a but here.

This week's chapter... changes everything. This series now has ROSE UP to the top. It's no longer just a classic childhood friend romance, this is now... something else. Something... peak. And I cannot believe YOM is going to be drawing this... this is already an instant classic, I can tell...

And my friend has graciously made me know that *White Album 2* is written by the same person... of course they do... and now this friend is also finishing *The Final Empire*, so I will know about what he's talking about soon...
