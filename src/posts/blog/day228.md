*Gimai Seikatsu* continues its tradition of showing Saki's POV of the things that has happened over the period of last few episodes. Her POV ran for almost half the episode, and at the end of her POV, she ask herself a question about her unpleasant feeling and try to describe it in one word. We then continue on and saw that Saki is now working on the same bookstore as Yuuta, and she gets to see Shiori in the flesh.

At the end of the episode, she answered that her feeling is indeed, "jealousy", towards Shiori. This romance is finally getting somewhere... this is the slowest burn romance I've ever watched, that's for certain. We shall see how this progress in the next few episodes until the end.

*Bye Bye Earth* this week is a chaotic mess as the whole army that went to the Katakombe gets slaughtered... we get the Mermaid imprinting on Belle and became super competent (really cool btw), and everyone coming together to meet their enemy, the Underdogs (I think that's their name). There's layers of stuff here that we weren't told, especially about how their fights are arranged into these "shows", like some kind of performance? Anyway, it seems like the fight has grown out of control for even the Underdogs, and Tiziano, a mermaid that was supposed to be on their side, has gone rogue and turned both the Upperdogs (the solist army that Belle is in) and the Underdogs troops into zombies.

It seems like they have to work together to survive, as their current group is too weak and filled with holes (because they need to have roles for some reason, these roles are not specifically explained but I'll just roll with it), so they have to combine into one. I usually enjoyed these kind of stuff, where two opposing factions worked together to fight one singular enemy, but the way they did it here... makes me want to see what happens in the novel instead, cause I think they're probably well explained there. Alas, the novel is not translated... rest in pepperonis...
