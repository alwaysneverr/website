*Spice & Wolf* groupwatch has return as we tackle the backlog of two episodes that was deliberately left there because we were warned of the "cliffhanger" that occur at episode 18... and boy oh boy the story gets spicier as Lawrence butts head with the fish lord, Amati. He has to seek the help of Diana, the mysterious lady from episodes ago, who holds pyrite that he needed. Honestly, the economy behind this "duel" of his with Amati is really fascinating, and I can't believe that they can create a plot out of this concept, even though empirically the duel is more like a game of hot potato in my opinion.

Anyway, the ending of ep 19... it's just... absolute cinema. Lawrence finally saying, or in this case, not really describing what he feels, since it's indescribable, which is perfect. Holo turns out have been trying to signal that she is working to undermine Amati this whole time, but Lawrence didn't trust her well enough, especially after the misunderstanding they have where Lawrence withheld the fact that Yoitsu, Holo's hometown, may not exist anymore from her. It's a whole drama, but I'm happy to learn that they have resolved it, and they even hug each other... man... so cute.
