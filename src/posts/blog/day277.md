I thought I had class today, which coincide with my AC getting maintained, but turns out I didn't have class at all... so I ended up spending my time watching *Joker: Folie à Deux* instead...

I'd say I'm a bit mixed about it. The music are awesome, everyone can fucking sing that's for sure... the plot though... uhhh... there's not a lot of it? I mean, I guess we are seeing Arthur filling the cracks in his psyche with songs, and this is him healing, even if at the end... well, I'm not gonna spoil you about it, even if you don't care about the movie. Read a synopsis somewhere.

I just think it's a bit of a waste of my money, perhaps. The movie only managed to grab me in some moments, but at times it seemed like I was waiting for a shoe to drop, but it never did. It's like an inflating baloon(*sic*) that never really pops, just deflating slowly, depressingly.
