I somehow got it in me to watch six episodes of this series, but only because this series is so *fun*, and... well... I'm not really a stranger to this situation, knowing how much of a chuuni I was back when I'm in HIGH SCHOOL. YES, HIGH SCHOOL, NOT EVEN MIDDLE SCHOOL.
