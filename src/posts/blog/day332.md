Anything written before this day and after day 321 aren't written on the day it happens, nor does it properly represent what I feel.

You know what I feel right now? I feel like I'm all over the place. Angry, tired, depressed, trapped, any gloomy adjective you can think of, that's what I'm feeling right now. I found myself punching the steering wheel without any good reason today (because I couldn't find a parking spot to get my mother from the hospital).

It got me thinking, this isn't good. I haven't properly let out my emotion in a while. I might have been bottling it up, keeping it with me, carrying this burden. It got me thinking that I haven't updated this thing in a while.

This project should've been my box, the kind of box Jemma Simmons from Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D use to repress her undesirable emotion... but I find myself thinking for the past few months, I've been lying to myself and only putting the positive things that I've enjoyed. Mostly gacha rolls and thoughts about the anime I'm watching or the manga/manhwa/light novel I'm reading.

I should make this my private hideaway to voice out the thoughts I'm not saying out loud, not pretending that I'm some kind of weeb reviewer that think his thoughts are original enough to be shared to the world.

Who am I kidding, nobody is reading this. Not anymore. I should just go ahead and say what I want to say.

I hate this fucking paper that I'm supposed to do. I don't want to do it in the easy way that was outlined by this professor, only because it's not actually easy, just sounds like it is.

I don't know if I will live after my mom's death, as I'm sure my father would instantly be bedridden in despair after she's gone. I DON'T WANT TO DEAL WITH ALL OF THE FAMILY STUFF THAT WILL HAPPEN ONCE SHE'S GONE.

I agree with my aunt about my mom being the "buzzkill" for everything. Her logical argument is like a splash of cold water that drowns any opportunity or bright ideas you have. However... my aunt is not someone I will back if my mom is dead because of the things that she has done and the lack of parenting she has done for her kids, so much so that it brought grief to my own fucking family. I dread the day I have to face her and listen to hear plead for my help, because I know my answer would be a resounding no and I'll have to be the villain of her story. I'm worried that my sister will have my mother's enormous capacity of empathy and support my aunt in her spiral of bizarre choices, so much so that she will make herself destitute to do so. I don't want her to turn to me for help after that happens.

I don't want to deal with my lack of emotional stability hindering me from confronting my own lack of feelings towards my girlfriend. Every day I hear my friends saying they want someone to be with and I get the feeling of guilt because I have someone, yet I do not appreciate it the way they would have. I FUCKING HATE IT. I feel like I'm making up stuff about her in my mind so that I have a reason to end things but I know that it's not true. I can't even tell anymore if her actions are something that points to that conclusion that I know instinctively or it's my own self-loathing creating situations that supported my darkest thoughts. I wouldn't be surprised if at the end I realized too late that I have hurted her, and I can't even claim that there is no other way. There ARE other ways to handle this yet I'm refusing to do it, or rather, refusing to acknowledge it existed. Five years of this, five years that I've wasted her time with my indecision. 

I've lost hope of ever getting into a proper web development job. Blowing up that interview was a wake up call to me, that I'm not cut out for this, and that thing I did a year ago was a fluke that would never occur again due to the nature of the circumstances at the time. I'm not actually worth that much money, and time is running out as I'm aging out for the intership(*sic*) period. Not one single job offer here will look at me once they realize I'm about to reach the age of 25. For me to graduate, I need to have at least one internship experience, or I'll be forced to move to a research track, and I think I have to start accepting the fact that I will be forced to move.

I think I've offended some people that I know, and I don't know how to handle it properly. I hate the fact that I'm not sure if this is my depressed thoughts making up stories in my mind about this, or it's something that actually happened and I have to figure out something. I hate hate hate hate hate having to figure out if I'm reading the room correctly or if I'm up my own ass too much about this situation. Because of this I started becoming angrier and angrier for these people, who as far as I know is probably too busy instead of actually mad at me, for not letting me know if I have a problem with them. I started looking at our every interaction in a bad light, thinking that I don't deserve being treated like I've crossed the line without them actually letting me know what lines I've crossed. I started feeling betrayed, because I thought whatever level of friendship we have is better than this treatment I'm receiving. And then I hated myself for thinking this because it could all be nothing and I've spiralled myself down into thinking this way, to not trust them and understand that they have their own life, their own problems, and their own business that they need to attend.

Lastly I'm tired of feeling tired. I'm tired of losing energy just as the sun sets because my mom would come in and lecture me about napping before sun sets being bad for your body. I'm tired of waking up at 5 AM everyday to pray. Hell, I'm tired of pretending I'm still a devout religion follower. But you know what? I'm scared even more that I'm going to miss this once it stopped.
