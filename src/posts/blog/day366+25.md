(Had to do it at this point, I want to make this a ritual)

The first hour of being twenty-five, I was playing Arknights.

The second and third hour of being twenty-five, I might be sleeping, or still playing Arknights.

The fourth hour of being twenty-five, I somehow was opening my phone to check something. Either I was trying to sleep then, or I woke up to do this. (Probably the former).

The fifth and sixth hour of being twenty-five, I was sleeping.

The seventh hour of being twenty-five, I woke up. My mom congratulate me on being twenty-five, but then goes on to berate me on being slow cause I still have my final exam today at eight o'clock.

The eighth hour of being twenty-five, I was zooming to my university for my last ever final exam on this building.

The ninth hour of being twenty-five, I was writing a story with my answers, my last poem for this godforsaken courses, the end of my days as a student after eight years. EIGHT YEARS OF THIS. I wasted so much of my time on this...

The tenth hour of being twenty-five, I come out of the class. There are some surprises in store, as my friend brought me a Spongebob t-shirt as a "birthday" gift, or rather, as an excuse to take a picture as a group of five, the group I ended up with after the tumultuous friendships I had during three years on this university.

The eleventh hour of being twenty-five, I spent on the road as we go to a mall to hang out for the last time as a college student.

The twelfth hour to the nineteenth hour of being twenty-five, I hung out with my friends, and buying them food, for the last time. I also spend it to go back home.

The twentieth hour of being twenty-five, I managed to get Narantuya from the limited banner, and had enough Gold Certificates to buy Suzuran off the Certificate shop.

The twenty-first hour of being twenty-five, I spent distracting myself from a present I was given by someone who loved me, but I have not loved back for years.

The twenty-second hour to the twenty fourth hour of being twenty-five, I went on rabbit holes after rabbit holes to avoid thinking about the future, my new future, that begins in [two weeks](/posts/intern/day1/).
