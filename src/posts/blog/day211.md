Honestly, I could have benefitted from watching *Deadpool 2*, because they definitely has some joke here that carried over from that movie... but anyway, glad that I did watch *Loki* so some of the TVA business just made sense to me!

This movie is just... pure fun, and I feel like they're the actual send-off, or a proper farewell, to the X-Men series that ends with Logan (I do not care if Dark Phoenix is the actual last one, I don't claim them), but also other properties by Marvel that are made by third party, like Blade, Elektra, and the unproduced Gambit movie starring Channing Tatum... oooh boy, he made his name for himself with this movie alright.

They also managed to bring Pyro back from X2 and X-Men: The Last Stand, and again, I wished that I watched at least those movies as well coming in, because I would feel more nostalgic about them, but without those I still enjoy the movie. The villain is... a bit goofy, not their best, but I think they aren't really the main focus of this movie...

Because really, the main focus is Deadpool himself, and WOLVERINE, BACK FROM THE DEAD- okay not really. As expected, this version is a washed up Wolverine who "failed" his X-Men by failing to be there while they got slaughtered. I feel a major Peter B. Parker vibes from this Wolverine, but without the wise mentor side to him, and it was a treat to see him grow from a real bitter jackass to someone that can play off Wade perfectly.

And I did not expect to find Deadpool/Wade to be full of heart this time around, as the catalyst of this movie stems from the fact that he is about to lose all of his friends, and it led him to recruit this Wolverine. Wade shows some genuinely emotional moments this time around, especially that time where he has no quip to respond to Wolvie's barrage of emotional insults after he discovered that he basically got duped by Wade without a real possibility to save his own world. Wade's "I'm going to fight you now" just sounds so chilling...
