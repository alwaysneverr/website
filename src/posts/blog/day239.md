A few months has passed since the tragic event where I was unable to finish my contracts in time...

The contracts event lives on, leaving me behind in its trail, as they recently reached the end of the spring (and summer?) contract season. However... a glitter of hope remains for me to join the season before it's over, as I try to do an aid contracts!

Aid contracts are contracts that were offered to the participants of the event from a pool of contractees who didn't finish in time (I'm assuming they determine if the contractee is still active first before offering these), and they will raffle these off to those who wants it. I tried my hands on it after inquiring if I can do that after being penalized last season (I was given the OK), and I was able to snag two series, the maximum you can get for aids.

*Dark Gathering* and *Ascendance of a Bookworm*. One is an ~~anime~~ manga series, and one is a Light Novel series. *Ascendance of a Bookworm* is actually a long series, comparable to *Mushoku Tensei* in terms of length. I'm excited to get my hands on these, and write reviews for it. I hope I can finish these before my college starts, though... crossing my fingers!

I binged *ATRI* anime today until I caught up, as promised after I finished the VN. The anime is a really good adaptation, and I must specify that it is an *adaptation* of the story, since they pretty much added a lot of things while keeping to the general main plot, and of course, as usual, not adapt some of the slower parts (for this one, I will say it's good that they did, but then I realized that I kinda miss those Slice of Life moments... but nevertheless, still something good that they did to get us to care about the main plot quicker).

I'd say the OP is probably a step down from the VN, but either way it has its own charm, just like every character here too! They feel different from the VN, but the core of their character is still there. It's a lot more fun seeing most of their antics animated compared to the VN, but they also have to cut a lot of stuff and change things around, which somehow makes it more interesting, because I don't know what else they would change (and how far they would change things without straying too far from the ending... or would they even have the same ending, knowing how the VN works?)

Speaking of endings, the VN has choices that the anime adapted rather well, in my opinion, and I get what they were doing. I wonder how they would do the other choices, because there's another one coming later in the story, which would lead to a different ending... I really wonder how they're going to do it.
