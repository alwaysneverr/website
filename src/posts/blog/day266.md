*ATRI* moves around its plot to finally reach the climax I've seen in the VN... along with finally seeding in the depressing truth about ATRI's lifespan... not much I can say about this, since I've talked much about the plot beforehand in VN, but I'd say that the anime worked around the story and makes it so that it doesn't give you this crushing feeling that simmers constantly in the background when I played the VN, if that makes sense...

She didn't really appreciate that and give him a slushie, because she's been adamant over taking the leadership role, and Nukkun has let her do her thing for a bit, even though he knows she's not ready for it.
