<summary>Honkai Star Rail 2.5 Story One-Day Speedrun</summary>

I began my *Honkai Star Rail* 2.5 Story One-Day Speedrun today, as I realized I had too much things I need to get before the new patch update in a few days...

Anyway, I got through the Hoolay part, that I absolutely hate not because of the quality, but because of how hateable Hoolay is as a character, which should be a compliment. Wolf creed this, foxian slave that, how about you eat this blade swung by the disciple of the person who put you in jail, get fucking rekt, hoolay.

Basically, the plot goes like this. Hoolay and Jiaoqiu gets into a yapping match about their backstory as some part of exchange to gather information regarding Hoolay's immortality and Feixiao's weakness. 

In this part of the story we get to see some red word choice, a dangerous choice that would lead us to kill random NPC if we picked them (I had to spoil myself for this one, I *hated* Hoolay, and therefore I'm gonna deprive him from eating some hapless people if I can help it). 

We get tons of exposition about Foxian and Borisin, and then some cool moments with Feixiao as we basically enter her demo time, carrying every enemy like a hurricane and collecting them so we can kill them with one battle... if I have Acheron on my team, I could even skip all of this with one stroke of her blade! Crazy ability...

The story also ramps up as the hunt gets to its climax, with the Xianzhou side deliberately baited Hoolay into attempting to crash the Wardance on the Skysplitter, a ship in the sky, to ambush him. But he turned the table on them by sacrificing himself and clawed his heart out, and it turns into a red moon(?) that threatens to induce Moon Rage on all foxians on the Xianzhou Loufu as a whole. 

Feixiao sacrificed herself and swallowed the Hoolay-turned-moon, thereby completing the rites of Borisin Warheads. However, Hoolay's will overwhelms her as she turned heel, forcing the team of Yanqing, Yunli, and March 7th to hold her off while she has a battle within herself with a manifestation of her darker desires, controlled by Hoolay.

I'd say, this story is pretty good, for a "filler" Xianzhou patch in between main chapters. The last filler patch was so meh that I have to drop the game for a bit (Heliobus story), but this one... this one got meat to it.

And I don't just mean the story involving Hoolay. Even the Wardance event concerning Luka (which is equivalent to the ghostbusting event) has way better story this time around. Not knocking on the Ghostbuster event though, they're actually pretty good as well, along with Aurum Alley event... or the Pokemon event... hell, they have a good track record for these "events" that end up on their archive ("Conventional Memoir"), like the Museum event, the previous patch event involving March 7th, the Ruan Mei critter mix and match event, and so much more...
