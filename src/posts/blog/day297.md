Took me a week to go back to *Chuunibyou demo Koi ga Shitai!* again, only because of the time I devoted to chase after the Stellar Jades in *Honkai: Star Rail*... and catching up with the *Babel* story before the event ended- anyway...

As I suspected, Rikka did see Yuuta back in her vulnerable state, after her father dies and her mother just abandons her to his father's family, and his grandfather who loathes her chuuni ways that she uses to substitute for her grief, unlike how moody and bitter he is, the old man.

I hate to say this but I had to agree with Yuuta on this one, it's pretty clear that this chuuni act is her ways to cope with the situation, and if they really want to get her to stop, they should have probably... let her face the reality. I understand if they want to let her down gently and protect her from getting hurt by not letting her know about their old home being sold, but don't they understand that by doing that, they only prolong whatever process she is doing right now, the things that they seem to absolutely abhor?

I mean, I'm not gonna say that Touka abhors this, it seems at times she enjoys entertaining Rikka in this delusion, but it's silly that she lets out her frustration once the delusion is getting torn down, right?

-----

I finished *Chuunibyou demo Koi ga Shitai!*. The thoughts I wrote above are my thoughts after watching episode 7 and 8, and now that it's over, I want to basically write something about this. I might actually put all of this into my contracts review at some point.

It's just that...

The world... it's getting crazier these days.

I've seen people that I thought all my life is the bastion of logic starting to break down and believe in the most outlandish thing I've ever heard.

Being crazy just doesn't feel... *crazy*, anymore, if that makes sense. Nowadays everyone can be their own little crazy, and people will respond in kind with their own brand of craziness. After finishing *Chuunibyou*, I started to get into these... spiralling thoughts, especially aimed at the characters in the series, and perhaps even in the real world, that kept taking away the agency that belongs to someone in order to conform to what they think is reality.

It's even more infuriating when I see this coming from Rikka's family. I still give Touka a pass because for all the big talk she has, she actually still plays some part in "enabling" Rikka's delusions, but what rights does anyone else has in tearing down the wall that someone has built in order to cope with the bad cards they are dealt with in life?

Do you think the person that you're griefing on would like it when you treat their daughter so harshly? Is the anger you have for her is an outlet for your anger of the unfairness of the situation? Like... what *rights* do you have?

I started to feel irrationally mad at the circumstances happening at the last two episodes of *Chuunibyou*. I started to get mad that I even began to think about Umineko, that deals with a similar theme where delusions, or in their case, their belief in magic and the truths that they know of the person they love trumps the cruel reality that threatens to not only break her, but break the truths that she believes in.

If Ange wants all of her family back, Rikka just want her dad back.

It makes me think of what I would do in case that I get dealt the bad card. As of right now, I already felt like I'm living my Chuuni side, that I categorize as my "online" persona. Al, the person that I am online, is my Dark Flame Master. Would I retreat to my delusions completely once I face the same difficulties as these two heroines (Ange and Rikka)? Would there be forces just like Touka or Rikka's grandfather, or the Speculative Goats, that would make me crumble and read the room?

God, this shit even starts making me think about *Imosae* as well. Would I lose control of the story that has me as its protagonist? Would I stay forever in the bad ending, spiralling into oblivion, as my story loses its meaning to anyone who wants to read it? I can even tie this to *Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint*. Is this the end of the Oldest Dream? Does my existence required to be so... gray? Just like how the *Chuunibyou* anime become once Rikka starts to become normal again...

I wish I can become the protagonist that breaks through the white and black spiral (unrelated to Uzumaki) of thoughts I trap myself in, and introduces back some color to my otherwise colorless and dull life. But here I am, sitting in front of the computer, hopped up on caffeine, getting myself mad each second as I realized how powerless I am.

*White Album 2* continues in *Closing Chapter* as Haruki entered his third year in university, and he's now in proximity of two girls (maybe three with Koharu but she's not here yet, he does have some juniors that likes him called Yada...). Chiaki, that has been endlessly mentioned by my benefactor/sponsor/contractor for this series as the perfect college girl friend (mind the spacing) you can have, and Mari, an onee-san office lady that plays the part of Haruki's boss. He also live alone in an apartment complex that would make youth today jealous.

Isn't that just grand? He's a working hard man, who doesn't have a girlfriend now, and he's not just working one job, it's actually two jobs, or even more, if I understand the implication of his conversation-

Yeah, you heard me right. After that ending of *Introductory Chapter* leaving us in a massive cliffhanger over whether or not Haruki will take Setsuna's offer, we finally get the answer. They're not together. I'm just going to assume that this isn't the full story, however, because there's a three year timeskip between the events, which led me to believe that something happened in between that gets him to be in this state, where he's completely absorbed in making sure he is busy doing something (preferrably work or study) and not let himself stop.

Just like Setsuna, who now refuses to sing... what happens between them? Seems like that's the intrigue we will see for this part of the story.

Story comments aside, I must say, Chiaki's sprite alone, in comparison to pretty much anyone, even the main two, is a MAJOR upgrade. Like, she's really beautiful. I don't get how they manage to make one character look really good, and then the rest has this inconsistencies where sometimes I feel a bit weirded out seeing the sprite that appears?

Hell, she looks better than all of the appearances I've seen her before this, which happens to be ~~a scene of what I assume is the ending part of the anime where they tease Closing Chapter but will never actually adapt it~~ a scene from the OP of Closing Chapter... damn.

I have to admit, I've not been putting my thoughts about *White Album 2* completely on this blog, as one part of the hole left behind by my inability to keep up with daily blog posts includes the days that I read *Introductory Chapter*. The only thoughts I have put in is the beginning parts and the ending, and I'm hoping to fill the gap before I forgot completely about what I feel during those reading I have.
