I finally finished *ATRI*...

<img src="/assets/images/posts/day238-1.webp" title="ATRI and Natsuki, seeing a part of the world he saved" alt="ATRI and Natsuki, seeing a part of the world he saved"/>

It was a ride through and through, every single toaster/scrap metal joke, the budding relationship between ATRI and Natsuki, and the progression of Natsuki's character as he find himself again while helping everyone around him... with the help of a very cheerful robot, though.

The story I feel are emotional at parts, but by the end I no longer feel sad at the inevitable conclusion, but rather, a sense of contentment. I wouldn't dare say this is peace but it sure seems like it.

<img src="/assets/images/posts/day238-2.webp" title="ATRI Fin" alt="ATRI Fin"/>

With this over though, I really am glad that I managed to finish this before the Anime is over, so I'm planning to binge the anime tomorrow to catch up. This will bring the number of seasonal anime I'm watching this season to a whooping EIGHT, a feat that I didn't think possible, but since I've been adding more and more anime to the mix (like Makeine and Bye Bye Earth) it's starting to become possible...

Next season I will NOT be watching any seasonals other than the one I've planned... (Blue Box and 365 Days to Wedding)
