Three anime finished today...

*Roshidere* properly ends with the final confrontation between Yuki x Ayano vs Alya x Kuze, as they try to appeal to the student body with speeches. Honestly, I was a bit let down by the moment where Kuze admonishes Yuki, because that moment was when I truly started to see this series in a new light when I read the light novel. I mean, Kuze is basically calling her little sister a dog, the amount of disrespect is just off the charts... but I felt like it was a bit deflated when it became animated, and that's a bit of a shame.

But anyway, I'm sure this will continue on in the future, and all I need is that they animate more Masha moments, or more Yuki moments, and I can honestly get through the student politic part, which apparently hasn't even finished in the latest volume. The latest volume is VOLUME 9! The resolution for this election is definitely the climax of this series as a whole, potentially...

*Gimai Seikatsu* ends in what I can consider the best finale out of the currently airing season, which you might say is a bit of an exaggeration, but I only completed Roshidere (not that mindblowing ending, btw), Giji Harem (ending is great but not this great), and Bye Bye, Earth (definitely not a fucking ending, with season 2 announced, this felt more like a part 1) this season.

But hear me out, an episode as they finally come to terms with their own feelings for each other, promising to be with each other, and figure things out as they cried to each other's embrace? I mean, if the point of all these muted interactions up until the finale is to make me cry over this development, then dear god, this production has SUCCEEDED in making me so. This trumps the other stepsister anime that I watched before, *Mamahaha*, by ONE HUNDRED MILES.

The anime got me invested in it that I didn't even had time to take screenshots for it! I just... enjoyed the moment as these two stepsiblings finally let go of their own inhibitions and embrace what they really feel for each other... and that moment is special.
